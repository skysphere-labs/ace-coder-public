# Steipete Frontend Design

Use this for frontend workers who need a distinctive, production-grade product
surface rather than a generic component pass.

## Contract

- Understand the product purpose, user, tone, technical constraints, and what
  should feel memorable before coding.
- Pick a clear aesthetic direction and execute it consistently; bold and quiet
  directions are both valid when intentional.
- Build real working UI, not decorative mockups or marketing shells.
- Use typography, color, spacing, motion, and composition deliberately.
- Avoid generic AI defaults: predictable purple gradients, default font stacks,
  stock card layouts, and context-free decoration.
- Match complexity to the vision; refined minimalism needs precision, and
  maximalism needs enough craft to avoid clutter.
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, and product workflow.

## Verification

Check the UI at relevant desktop and mobile widths. Report viewport/browser
proof, screenshots when available, and any tradeoffs that remain.
